TTRIBE

Your Vibe. Your Tribe.

“TTRIBE is not a clothing brand. It’s a reflection of who you are — without explanation.
Built on the idea that what you wear is not just seen, but felt, TTRIBE transforms everyday T-shirts into statements of identity. No noise. No chaos. Just clarity.”

01 / Why TTRIBE name?
The name is built on identity and belonging. The extra “T” transforms a familiar word into a memorable, ownable brand statement.
02 / Mission, Vision & Youth Culture
“Our aim is not to dress people — it is to help them feel seen. Where clothing becomes a reflection of identity, and every individual feels confident.”
03 / The Challenge & Streetwear Lookbook
Designing an iconic brand mark carrying recall without loud visuals, backed by an authentic 8-poster editorial lookbook wall collage.
04 / Brand Typeface & Identity System
Restrained geometric grotesque typography paired with tactile concrete stone stationery, communicating authenticity through precision.
05 / Brand Values & Retail Presence
Mind map architecture linking Belonging, Structure, Luxury, Strength, Distinction, and Modernity to the physical store experience.
06 / Monogram, Packaging & Merchandise
The monochromatic black & white system deployed across shopping bags, canvas tote bags, custom stickers, pins, and kraft swing tags.
